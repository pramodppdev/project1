    How to play
    • Click a tile to inspect it. Tiles with a dashed outline border your land and can be claimed for gold.
    • Build on your empty tiles. Each terrain allows different buildings: farms on plains, lumber camps in forests, quarries on hills, gold mines in the mountains.
    • Every production building needs one worker. If you have fewer people than buildings, output drops.
    • People eat food and pay taxes. With spare food and housing your population grows each turn. Build houses to raise the cap.
    • Build barracks to train soldiers. Soldiers defend all your land and can attack neighbouring enemy tiles, one attack per turn.
    • Capture the Crimson Horde's capital 🏰 to win. Lose your own and the game is over.
